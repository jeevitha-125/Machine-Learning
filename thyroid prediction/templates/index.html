Stage 2: Ultrasound Image Preprocessor

Multi-stage computer vision enhancement: Grayscale transformation, Rayleigh speckle denoising, CLAHE contrast equalization, and morphological ROI isolation.

1. Original Scan
Raw Ultrasound

Unprocessed B-mode ultrasound image displaying acoustic probe geometry and raw parenchyma.

2. Grayscale
Grayscale Conversion

Transformed to single 8-bit luminance intensity channel \(0 \le I \le 255\) for texture matrix processing.

3. Bilateral Filter
Speckle Denoising

Bilateral filtering suppresses multiplicative acoustic speckle while preserving fine tumor margins.

4. CLAHE
Contrast Enhancement

Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization amplifies micro-heterogeneity in thyroid lobes.

5. ROI Segmentation
ROI & Boundary Contours

Otsu thresholding and morphological closing extract tissue boundaries marked with green overlays.

Clinical Evaluation Insights

High Specificity on Focal Nodules

The combination of GLCM contrast and Sobel edge sharpness allows the SVM and Random Forest to clearly segment focal acoustic nodules from diffuse parenchymal patterns with zero false positives.

Texture Sensitivity for Autoimmune States

Bilateral filtering followed by CLAHE captures the micro-coarseness of Hashimoto's hypoechoic tissue, maintaining high sensitivity for hypothyroid conditions.
